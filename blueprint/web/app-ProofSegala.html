Z.3 Proof of Theorem 3

In order to extend Segala’s result to liveness, we need to display how a scheduler of \(\mathcal{P}\) can be translated into a scheduler of \(\mathcal{P}'\). Unfortunately, Segala’s proof does not follow that route [ Seg95b ] . Indeed, this construction is not trivial at all. Thus, we are going to split it into multiple steps corresponding to the three main difficulties: weak transitions, simulation by probability distribution and translating \(\mathcal{P}\) to \(\mathcal{P}'\).

Z.3.1 Weak Transitions

Definition 25 Weak Closure
#

The weak closure of a PLTS \(\mathcal{P}\) is a PLTS, denoted \(\mathcal{P}^w\), where transitions are replaced by weak transitions.

Lemma 24
#

Let \(\mathcal{P}\) be a PLTS, \(\text{tdist}(\mathcal{P}) = \text{tdist}(\mathcal{P}^w)\).

Proof ▶

The inclusion \(\subseteq \) is trivial because any transition of \(\mathcal{P}\) is a weak transition so schedulers of \(\mathcal{P}\) are schedulers of \(\mathcal{P}'\). However, the other inclusion \(\supseteq \) is more tricky. [Done in Lean]

Z.3.2 Distribution Monad

Definition 26 Distribution Lifting
#

The distribution lifting of a PLTS \(\mathcal{P}\) is the PLTS defined as \(\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{P}) := (\mathcal{D}(Q), \delta _{q_\star }, L, \mathcal{D}(T))\) where

\[ (\mu ,l,\mathfrak {m}) \in \mathcal{D}(T) \iff \exists \mu ' \in \mathcal{D}(Q), (\mu ,l,\mu ') \in H \wedge \sum _{\mu }\mathfrak {m}(\mu )\cdot \mu = \mu ' \]
Lemma 25
#

Let \(\mathcal{P}\) be a PLTS, \(\text{tdist}(\mathcal{P}) = \text{tdist}(\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{P}))\).

Proof ▶

The inclusion \(\subseteq \) is trivial because Dirac distributions allow to encode any scheduler on \(\mathcal{P}\) as a scheduler on \(\mathcal{P}'\). However, the other inclusion \(\supseteq \) is more tricky. [Done in Lean]

Z.3.3 Strong Probabilistic Simulation

Definition 27 Strong Probabilistic Simulation
#

A strong probabilistic simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{P}'\) is a relation \(\mathcal{S}\subseteq Q \times Q'\) such that \((q_\star ,q'_\star ) \in \mathcal{S}\) and, for all \((q,q') \in \mathcal{S}\) and \((q,l,\mu ) \in T\), there exists \((q',l,\mu ') \in T'\) such that \((\mu ,\mu ') \in \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{S})\).

Lemma 26
#

Let \(\mathcal{P}\) and \(\mathcal{P}'\) be two PLTSs, there exists a probabilistic forward simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{P}'\), if and only if, there exists a strong probabilistic simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{P}'^w)\).

Proof ▶

This proof is purely mechanical. It suffices to consider \(\mathcal{S}\) a probabilistic forward simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{P}'\) (resp. a strong probabilistic simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{P}'^w)\)) and check that it is a strong probabilistic simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{P}'^w)\) (resp. a probabilistic forward simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{P}'\)). [Done in Lean]

Lemma 27
#

Let \(\mathcal{P}\) and \(\mathcal{P}'\) be two PLTSs, if there exists a strong probabilistic simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{P}'\) then \(\text{tdist}(\mathcal{P}) \subseteq \text{tdist}(\mathcal{P}')\).

Proof ▶

This is a simplified version of the original soundness proof which is tractable to formalize in Lean. [Done in Lean]

Theorem 28
#

Let \(\mathcal{P}\) and \(\mathcal{P}'\) be two PLTSs, if there exists a probabilistic forward simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{P}'\) then \(\text{tdist}(\mathcal{P}) \subseteq \text{tdist}(\mathcal{P}')\).

Proof ▶

If there exists a probabilistic forward simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{P}'\) then, by Lemma 26, there exists a strong probabilistic simulation of \(\mathcal{P}\) by \(\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{P}'^w)\) so, by Lemma 27, 25 and 24,

\begin{equation*} \text{tdist}(\mathcal{P}) \subseteq \text{tdist}(\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{P}’^w)) = \text{tdist}(\mathcal{P}’^w) = \text{tdist}(\mathcal{P}’) \end{equation*}
